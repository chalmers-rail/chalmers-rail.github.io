Outreach

Science communication, media coverage, public exhibitions, and panel discussions.

We regularly contribute to science communication and outreach activities related to robotics and physical AI — through media appearances, public demonstrations, panel discussions, and science festivals. If you are interested in collaborating on an outreach activity or inviting us to speak, please get in touch.
